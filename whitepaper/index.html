1. What CRAIC is

Craic Coin ($CRAIC) is a community coin on the Solana blockchain, themed on the Irish idea of "craic": good fun, good company and a good night out. It is the native token of the Craic Hub Exchange, where you get 25% off when you pay in $CRAIC, and of Craic Swap. It has no revenue share and no intrinsic value, and nobody is promised anything for holding it.

2. Utility: Craic Swap and Craic Hub

$CRAIC is built around two live tools. They are the centre of the project.

  • Craic Swap is a non-custodial swap page. Connect your own Solana wallet and swap SOL, USDC, USDT, JUP, BONK or almost any Solana token for $CRAIC through Jupiter. A cross-chain tab, powered by ChangeNOW, lets you bring coins such as BTC or ETH across to SOL first. Craic Swap never holds your coins or keys. Network and provider fees apply, including a small referral fee that supports Craic Swap.
  • Craic Hub listings, part of the Craic Hub Exchange, are a Solana token marketplace for memecoins and fresh launches. A basic listing is free, and a person checks every listing before it goes live. A featured slot at the top costs 0.25 SOL, or 25% less when you pay in $CRAIC. Holders can also see their holder rank and spend $CRAIC on boosts and flair.
  • Craic Bot is Craic Hub's Telegram bot, so devs can send in a listing without leaving Telegram.

The only official payment wallet is the Phantom wallet 7GrdQxfmgZMDjBYeivMwg94jqthLqcBuUG75nFd9mZpW. Using these tools is not an investment: there is no staking, yield, revenue share or promised return.

3. Story and mission

CRAIC launched on pump.fun on 14 October 2026 as a fair launch, with no presale and no tokens set aside for a team before launch. Everyone, the dev included, got tokens the same way, on the same public pump.fun bonding curve.

Mission: to be more than a meme by building a friendly, Irish-flavoured online community. That means quizzes, memes and banter that are free for anyone to join, run openly and safely.

4. The Craic Club

The Craic Club launched on 15 October 2026 in the Craic Chat on Telegram.

  • Craic Pub Quiz: every Friday at 8pm UK time in the chat. It's 10 questions on Irish trivia, music and sport, starting Friday 23 October 2026.
  • Meme of the Week: post your best Irish or pub-banter meme with #CraicMeme. The admins pick a winner, who gets announced at Friday's quiz and pinned.
  • XP leaderboard: earn XP for quiz answers, meme wins and being helpful. The top 10 is posted every Friday.

Always free to enter. You never need to buy, hold or send any token, SOL or money to take part. Prizes are currently shoutouts, pinned memes and bragging rights. No prize amounts are promised, and prizes are never a reason to buy anything. Admins will never DM you first or ask for a seed phrase, private key or "claim" link.

🧭 The path ahead

Our roadmap is centred on $CRAIC as the native coin of the Craic Hub Exchange. Each stage builds on the last. These are aims, with no dates.

Phase 1: Craic Hub Exchange live, with $CRAIC as its native coin (done). Swap any coin from your own wallet, launch a coin for 0.05 SOL total (25% less in $CRAIC), create a pool to pair any coin with any coin, and follow the live $CRAIC burn tracker.

Phase 2: Launch of the Craic Wallet (coming soon). Our own self-custody Solana wallet. You hold your own keys, and we never hold your funds. It is built to work with the Craic Hub Exchange and Craic Hub.

Phase 3: More $CRAIC utility on the exchange (aim). More exchange features and discounts paid in $CRAIC; graduation from the pump.fun bonding curve to full decentralised exchange liquidity; and broader aggregator coverage, including Jupiter verification.

Phase 4: Burn progress and more listings (aim). Fees paid in $CRAIC on the exchange are burned, aiming for 250M $CRAIC burned. More tokens listed on Craic Hub and the exchange; CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap applications; and larger exchanges, as an aim.

These are goals we are working towards. Nothing here is confirmed or guaranteed, and we do not commit to dates.
